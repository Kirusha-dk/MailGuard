# v48 calibrated sparse baseline

Goal: at least 90% spam recall and at most 100 false positives across 50,000
total messages. No assumption that exactly 25,000 messages are ham.

v47 used capped source-held-out OOF data and exported a refit estimator with
new hard-example weights under the old OOF threshold. Its reported scores
therefore did not evaluate the exported estimator. Its global structural
scaler also saw held-out feature distributions. v48 establishes a measurable
baseline without those mismatches before introducing another neural ensemble.

- Use full frozen TRAIN, with no source/class cap or synthetic additions.
- Strip source headers through v47 MIME canonicalization; retain visible domains.
- Remove exact canonical subject/body duplicates across splits; count conflicts.
  Near-duplicate campaign leakage is not ruled out by this exact-content check.
- Fit sparse character/word TF-IDF features on TRAIN only.
- Choose one of three regularization strengths on half of validation.
- Calibrate its exact FP breakpoint on the other half; include score ties.
- Keep the selected model unchanged after calibration.
- Reserve 40% of the proportional development FP budget as margin. This is
  heuristic margin, not a statistical guarantee for future traffic.
- Score the reused frozen TEST, report actual counts and per-source results,
  and save hash-only per-message scores/FP/FN for local error analysis.

Run `python3 tools/benchmark_v48_calibrated.py` for 1000 rows per split;
use `--limit 0` for full evaluation. Canonical dedup can reduce TEST below
50,000, in which case the full 50k gate is explicitly false. Do not refill
from training or duplicate rows to reach the requested count.

This is a standalone content classifier, not the deployed Rspamd ensemble.
The historical test has already been inspected; even a passing result is
engineering evidence only. A recent independent labeled 50k sample is still
required for the stability claim. Pipeline success does not mean quality passed.
