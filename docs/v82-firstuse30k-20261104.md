# Locked v82 first-use English spam audit, 4 November 2026

This evaluation answers a spam-detection question on 30,000 previously unscored
English messages. It cannot measure false positives on ordinary mail: the source
contains spam-trap messages only. It is not general email accuracy.

The checkpoint is frozen at step8000, SHA256
`1566901303dde37cbbeec93e22cc5d513e3bc2248dff79e6a0f171215f6a197f`.
The threshold is the original4 November SELECT threshold0.03910822421312332.
Neither weights nor threshold are selected or changed using this cohort.

Source: [Bruce Guenter's public spam archive](https://untroubled.org/spam/),
year2012, SHA256 `65bdb680fe95f3d2f52d24d99af7b7d6490c03fc5508313e8f5edefb4672de41`.
The publisher permits research use without restriction. Labels are inherited
from a spam trap and have not been individually adjudicated. Other years from
this provider were used previously; this is not a new-publisher evaluation.
Year2012 is absent from both earlier500k benchmark source inventories.

Before inference, the script screens:

- The verified v64 legacy exclusion database,3,486,848 content keys. Its audit
  includes earlier training/development/test pools, the earlier500k corpus,
 75,271 HAM messages and the complete pinned seven-email source.
- All500,000 subsequently evaluated v64 transfer rows, with verified shard hashes.
- All frozen v79/v80 TRAIN, SELECT, CAL, TEST and fresh-test identities/families,
  checking the current TRAIN/SELECT fingerprints against the locked checkpoint.
- Full canonical content, raw bytes, normalized templates, campaign-prefix and
  prepared-view fingerprints using the established legacy parsers, plus the
  v82 canonical identities and45-word prefix families.

Files are ordered by a predeclared hash of seed and archive-relative filename.
Only MIME mail with raw size30..250000 bytes, supported English text and pinned
fastText en>=0.8 is eligible. The first30,000 surviving distinct identities and
families are frozen before model initialization. A shortage fails rather than
refilling from old data. This is a filtered, diverse historical cohort, not a
random sample of contemporary traffic. Semantic near-duplicates and unknown
pretraining exposure cannot be completely ruled out.

Inference uses the original v82 text normalization and head/tail tokenizer on
CPU, with eval/no_grad. Checkpoint, parameter hash, RNG and absence of gradients
are checked; no optimizer is created. The monthly training branch is unchanged.

The workflow uploads only counts, hashes, origins and scores. Public email text
is temporary on the runner and is not included in Git or evaluation artifacts.
Once completed, this cohort is consumed as a test; replays are regressions,
not additional first-use evaluations.

Code:tools/evaluate_v82_firstuse30k.py.
Workflow:.github/workflows/v82-firstuse30k.yml, read-only experiment branch
mailguard-v82-external-20261102. Results will be linked from the verified report.
