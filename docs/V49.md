# v49: NB-weighted text experts

The v48 frozen model scored 92.71% recall, 74 FP and 2647 FN on 81,688
public historical rows. This is the starting point, not evidence of 98% quality.
The available disjoint-content pool is smaller than the requested 100,000.

v49 evaluates NB log-likelihood weighted character and word features with
regularized logistic regression. NB ratios and TF-IDF are fit on TRAIN only.
Four fixed candidates and the original v48 estimator compete on the model
selection half of validation; the chosen estimator's threshold is calibrated
on the separate calibration half. No estimator is refit after calibration.

The new target is 98% spam recall and at most 100 FP across 100,000 total mail.
The development FP budget is 60% of that proportional rate. This margin is
heuristic, not a guarantee. Reports always contain actual counts and FP rates.

The previously inspected 81,688 rows are now engineering evaluation only.
Their labels are not used for training, selection, or threshold calibration.
Per-source errors are summarized to identify weak domains, without adding
test failures to training. Source selection in the public pool is historical
and exact canonical dedup does not exclude near-identical campaigns.

`--limit 1000` is the default quick iteration. The workflow runs this first,
then full training and fixed-model comparison. A successful Actions job only
means execution succeeded; the JSON quality goal reports acceptance separately.
The public-pool evaluator cannot claim a 100k pass if fewer rows are available.

This branch remains an experimental standalone content classifier. A production
Rspamd integration and recent customer test remain necessary for autonomous use.
